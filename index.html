What we look for

Five qualities. Verified, not assumed.

01
Ethics
Plain dealing. Transparent quotes. Honoring the contract — and the handshake before it.
02
Skill
Craft you can run a hand across. Crews trained on the materials they're being asked to install.
03
Reliability
Showing up. Calling back. Closing the punch list before the invoice.
04
Consistency
The job in March looks like the job in November. No drift between the showroom and the install.
05
Creativity
Pattern, layout, and material choices that make the room — not just cover the subfloor.
The NFA Stamp of Approval

It is not a sticker. It is a site visit.

Before a member is approved, our team walks their warehouse, audits their paperwork, and observes a job in progress. The stamp says we've been there in person.

A

Licensing & Insurance

Active Nevada State Contractors Board license at the correct monetary tier; current general liability and workers' comp on file.

B

Site Safety

OSHA-aligned jobsite practices, dust containment, respiratory PPE, and proper handling of adhesives, sealers, and tear-out.

C

Operational Quality

Written contracts, change-order discipline, a real warranty policy, and a means of resolving disputes before they escalate.

D

Workmanship

An NFA reviewer observes a live install or recently completed job. Tolerances, transitions, and finish are inspected against trade standards.
